The Stratigraphic Archive Field Manual

Curatorial Reference, Preparation Standards & Ethical Field Treatise // Edition VIII
REAL-WORLD STEWARDSHIP PROCLAMATION: LEAVE NO TRACE
The Stratigraphic Archive: Field Paleontologist is an educational simulation. In the real world, paleontological fieldwork requires strict legal permits, scientific methodology, and profound respect for natural landscapes. True paleontologists practice LEAVE NO TRACE: never surface collect or quarry on public, indigenous, or protected lands without verified legal permits; leave geological sites pristine; and ensure every scientifically significant discovery is accessioned into permanent public museum care for future generations.
DISCLAIMER: This game is an independent educational simulation. It is neither officially endorsed by nor formally affiliated with any of the museums, universities, or foundations cited. Authentic donation links are provided throughout to directly support real-world scientific research and public education.
Volume I: Stratigraphic Facies & Field Logistics
Sedimentary basins preserve biotic history through differential deposition. Coarse fluvial sandstones (such as the basal channel sands of Hell Creek or the Morrison) typically preserve robust, heavily mineralized vertebrate teeth and large limb bonebeds. Conversely, quiet anoxic lacustrine shale beds (Burgess Shale, Messel, Yixian) foster Lagerstätten preservation, retaining carbonized feathers, eyes, and skin halos.
Moving your field team between international basins incurs significant transport logistics and regional permitting fees. Travel costs scale by distance across the 8 global bio-provinces. Securing a Curatorial Charter by donating Crown Jewels to regional institutions reduces local transit and permit fees to $0 permanently.

Volume III: Public Repositories & The Anti-Idle Economy
Field paleontology balances immediate commercial liquidity against permanent scientific prestige:
1. The Commercial Auction Floor: Sells specimens to private collectors and syndicates. Yields immediate multi-million cash injections for outfitter gear, but forfeits perpetual research funding.
2. Public Repository Donation: Accessions specimens into public trust (Smithsonian, NHM London, AMNH, IVPP, etc.). Rather than an uncapped background money fountain that exploits sleep, public donations are split into a balanced two-tier economic system:
  • Per-Dig Field Research Grant: Affiliated museums match active field excavations, paying out a direct cash grant every time you drill a stratigraphic core.
  • The 45-Minute Curatorial Escrow Cap: Passive background stipends accrue into an uncollected escrow vault. This vault caps out at exactly 45 minutes of real time (180 ticks), requiring active curators to return and claim their research allocation.
  • Regional Curatorial Charters: Donating a Tier 5 Crown Jewel drops all travel and boundary permit fees across that entire bio-province to $0 permanently.
